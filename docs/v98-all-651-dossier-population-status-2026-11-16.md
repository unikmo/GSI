## Contents of each case

1. **Investor** — sovereign/private investor group and reported investing entity; confirmed vehicle if evidence independently reviewed, otherwise unverified vehicle claim or null.
2. **Investee** — company/event and country; independently checked legal name only where supported.
3. **Deal** — announcement/closing status, subscribed amount claim with basis/currency, instrument, economics and staged transactions when source-backed.
4. **Finances** — pre-entry revenue/EBITDA with period and sources where available, other original financial claims retained with exact research source pointer and review status.
5. **Partners** — original PE syndicate references, source ownership claims, explicitly announced co-investors (never a whole-round amount mistaken for one participant).
6. **Advisers** — named issuer-disclosed lawyers and other transaction advisers where actually sourced, not fabricated from corporate relationships.
7. **Returns** — investor proceeds, MOIC and IRR only if investor-specific cash flows are substantiated; otherwise null.
8. **Evidence and open gaps** — issuer/exchange primary URLs, other source URLs, file path and source position for supplementary claims, claims copied without independent certification and named tasks to verify missing data.
