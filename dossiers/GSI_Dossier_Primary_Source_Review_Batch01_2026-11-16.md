**Strategic interpretation [INFERENCE]:** Mubadala seeks exposure to long-duration logistics/leasing platforms alongside specialist infrastructure managers. Alternative interpretation: a single portfolio-financing co-investment, not evidence of a broad shift to shipping-container assets.

**Decision use:** Mark investor-specific **announced US$300m**, but do not equate the announcement with money paid or classify it as Europe investment. Establish investment company domicile and instrument, effective settlement and legal investor vehicle.
